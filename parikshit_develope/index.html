Clinical Standards & Guidelines

Emergency Triage & Resource Allocation Guidelines

HopeLine adheres to the Emergency Severity Index (ESI) triage protocols, providing a standardized 5-tier triage classification to optimize emergency department resources.

ESI Level 1: Resuscitation

Immediate life-saving intervention required (cardiac arrest, severe trauma, respiratory failure). Direct admission to ICU Ventilator Bed.

ESI Level 2: Emergent

High risk situation, confused/lethargic, or severe pain. Priority bed allocation within 10–15 minutes with dedicated oxygen support.

ESI Level 3: Urgent

Patients requiring multiple diagnostic tests or specialized care with normal vitals. Enqueued for General Medical Ward bed allocation.
